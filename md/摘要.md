国内不少中小型玻璃企业的生产管理还停留在人工台账和电子表格阶段，工单靠手写、库存靠人盘，数据更新滞后，批次一旦出问题很难往回追溯。玻璃生产本身又是高温连续作业，原料配比对精度要求高，物料在各工序之间流转频繁，排产和仓储如果跟不上节奏，很容易出现断料或者积压。本文针对这类企业的实际状况，用Spring Boot加MyBatis搭后端，Vue.js加Element UI做前端，设计了一套前后端分离的生产管理系统。整个系统围绕生产工单来组织业务，从客户订单到生产计划再到工单排产，开工时按BOM自动展开生成领料出库单，报工后自动生成成品入库单，库存随单据变动实时更新。同时做了质检录入、不合格品处置和按批次号反向追溯的功能。对中小玻璃企业来说，这套方案落地成本不算高，业务流程也比较贴合实际的生产场景。

Many small and medium-sized glass manufacturers in China still rely on handwritten work orders and spreadsheets to manage production and inventory. Data updates lag behind actual operations, and tracing a defective batch back to its source is difficult. Glass production runs at high temperatures around the clock, demands precise raw-material proportioning, and involves frequent material movement between processes, so any delay in scheduling or warehousing can easily lead to material shortages or overstocking. This thesis addresses these problems by building a production management system with a separated front-end and back-end architecture, using Spring Boot and MyBatis on the server side and Vue.js with Element UI on the client side. The system is organized around production work orders. It converts customer orders into production plans and then into work orders. When a work order starts, the BOM is recursively expanded to generate a material-picking slip automatically. When production is reported complete, a finished-goods receipt is created and inventory quantities are updated in real time. Quality inspection, non-conforming material handling, and batch-level traceability are also included. For small and medium glass enterprises, this solution is relatively low-cost to deploy and fits their day-to-day production workflows.
